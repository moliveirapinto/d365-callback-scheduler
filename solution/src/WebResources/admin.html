Configure the scheduler

Avg. wait if you call now: …

These settings shape the experience on every public callback page — time-slot length, booking window, daily capacity and operating hours all flow through here. Adjust once, and customers see the change on their next visit.

1. Time-slot length

Every callback window will be exactly this long. Stored in env var cbk_SlotMinutes.

2. Workstream

Pick the proactive engagement config. Every booking is created against this workstream; its queue's operating hours define the bookable window. Stored in cbk_ProactiveEngagementConfigId.
 

3. Capacity per slot

When a window already has this many bookings, it disappears from the customer page. Stored in cbk_MaxCallsPerSlot.
Set to 1 if you only want one customer per window.

4. Multiple bookings per customer

When OFF, a customer who tries to book a second callback is shown a modal with their existing booking and offered to cancel it or keep it. Stored in cbk_AllowMultipleBookings.
Allow customers to schedule multiple callbacks If a customer already has an upcoming callback, a new attempt will trigger the duplicate dialog.

Operating hours

Read directly from the queue attached to your selected workstream.
Loading...
How slots are generated. The customer-facing pages look up the workstream you picked in step 2, follow it to its queue, then read that queue's Operating Hours record. The day's first slot starts at the queue's open time and the last slot ends at the close time (in slot-length steps from step 1). Days marked Closed are disabled. If the queue has no operating-hours record, the page falls back to 24/7 (slots from 00:00 to 23:59 every day).
